Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
